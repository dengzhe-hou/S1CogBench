Controlled latency

These are the accepted per-mode timings of the controlled latency study (P1). Times are milliseconds on one A100 40GB device at batch size one, with 20 warm-up calls per mode. N counts timed requests, and Tokens gives median input length. Ratio is the median per-request end-to-end ratio to Llama on matching source identities, using the original analyzer. Approved 200-request variants retain their prefix sizes. Forward and end-to-end timers are distinct from API service time (API_TIME_COST.md).

The protocol is in SUPPLEMENTARY_CONTROLS.md. Laya TileLang is the row the paper labels Laya fast. Its two largest P95 values, in Recent probes and SHJ, are consistent with compiling kernels for new input shapes during timed calls.
